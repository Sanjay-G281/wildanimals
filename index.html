Sounds of wild animals

Panther

A black panther is the melanistic colour variant of the leopard (Panthera pardus) and the jaguar (Panthera onca). Black panthers of both species have excess black pigments,
but their typical rosettes are also present. They have been documented mostly in tropical forests, with black leopards in Africa and Asia, and black jaguars in Central and South America. Melanism is caused by a recessive allele in the leopard, and by a dominant allele in the jaguar.

HoneyBadger

The honey badger is a small but powerful mammal known for its aggressive behavior and resistance to venom.
Honey badgers are known for their tenacity and fearlessness, often attacking animals much larger than themselves.

Polar Bear

Polar bears are both terrestrial and pagophilic (ice-living) and are considered marine mammals because of their dependence on marine ecosystems. They prefer the annual sea ice but live on land when the ice melts in the summer. They are mostly carnivorous and specialized for preying on seals, particularly ringed seals.

Monkey

Monkeys are primates of the family Cercopithecidae, and they are found in a variety of habitats across the world.

Eagle

Eagles are large birds of prey that belong to the family Accipitridae. They are known for their keen eyesight, powerful talons, and impressive hunting skills. Eagles are found in various habitats around the world, including mountains, forests, and open plains.
